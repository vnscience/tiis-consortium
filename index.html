Who We Are

Building a Global Community of Computing and IT Institutions

The Technology and Information Institutions Consortium (TIIS Consortium) connects higher education faculties, schools, and departments specializing in Computing, Information Technology, and Computer Science to share knowledge and build enduring institutional partnerships.

Beyond individual conferences, TIIS establishes a continuous academic platform supporting leadership dialogues, joint research groups, faculty and student mobility, and co-organized scientific forums.

Our Vision

To become a dynamic international network connecting computing and IT institutions worldwide, fostering academic excellence, meaningful partnerships, and innovation in higher education.

Our Mandate

Connecting institutions across regions to advance computing education, collaborative publications, academic exchange, and sustainable global partnerships.

About Us

Building a Global Community of Computing and IT Institutions

Proposed brand identity · tiis-consortium.org · Founding Academic Initiative.

The Technology and Information Institutions Consortium (TIIS Consortium) is an international academic network connecting higher education institutions, particularly faculties, schools, and departments specializing in Computing, Information Technology, Computer Science, and related disciplines.

TIIS provides a structured collaborative platform for academic leaders, faculty members, and researchers to exchange institutional experiences, develop joint research initiatives, support academic mobility, and co-organize international scientific events.

Activities

Connecting Institutions Through Meaningful Academic Engagement

Continuous interaction and academic cooperation across six core activity areas.

Institutional Networking & Meetings

Regular online and in-person meetings bring together university leaders, deans, and department heads. These dialogues align institutional priorities and identify new opportunities for cooperation.

University Visits & Exchange

Hosted institutional visits include campus tours, laboratory introductions, and bilateral academic discussions. These exchanges strengthen trust and allow units to share operational experiences.

International Academic Conferences

Member institutions are encouraged to jointly organize, host, and participate in international conferences and scientific events. Co-hosting expands scholarly visibility and global academic reach.

Research & Academic Collaboration

TIIS facilitates joint research projects, collaborative publications, and specialized research groups across member faculties. Researchers connect across borders to address emerging computing challenges.

Faculty & Student Exchange

The consortium promotes visiting scholar appointments, faculty mobility, student exchanges, and international internships. These programs enrich academic training and cross-cultural learning.

Workshops & Knowledge Sharing

Thematic workshops, webinars, and expert talks highlight emerging technologies and advances in computing education. Members share pedagogical best practices and curriculum innovations.
