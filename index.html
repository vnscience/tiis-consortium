01. ABOUT TIIS

Building a Global Community of Computing and IT Institutions

The Technology and Information Institutions Consortium (TIIS Consortium) is an international academic network established to connect higher education institutions, particularly faculties, schools, and departments specializing in Computing, Information Technology, Computer Science, and related fields.

TIIS provides a collaborative platform for academic leaders, faculty members, researchers, and institutions to exchange knowledge, share experiences, develop partnerships, and create meaningful opportunities for international cooperation.

Beyond institutional connections, TIIS aims to build a sustainable academic community where collaboration continues through regular meetings, institutional visits, joint academic activities, research initiatives, and international conferences.

01. ABOUT TIIS

Building a Global Community of Computing and IT Institutions

An international academic consortium fostering long-term institutional partnerships across computing, computer science, and information technology.

The Technology and Information Institutions Consortium (TIIS Consortium) is an international academic network established to connect higher education institutions, particularly faculties, schools, and departments specializing in Computing, Information Technology, Computer Science, and related fields.

TIIS provides a collaborative platform for academic leaders, faculty members, researchers, and institutions to exchange knowledge, share experiences, develop partnerships, and create meaningful opportunities for international cooperation.

Beyond institutional connections, TIIS aims to build a sustainable academic community where collaboration continues through regular meetings, institutional visits, joint academic activities, research initiatives, and international conferences.

Founding Initiative Notice

The name Technology and Information Institutions Consortium (TIIS Consortium) and proposed brand identity (tiis-consortium.org) represent a founding proposal prepared for formal review and ratification by participating academic institutions prior to official charter launch.

TIIS Consortium functions as an ongoing institutional collaboration network, distinct from independent international scientific conferences such as EIDT and SIMC.
