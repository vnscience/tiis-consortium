Membership

Become Part of a Global Academic Community

Open to universities, faculties, schools, and departments advancing computing and IT education and research.

Who Can Join?

  • 01 Faculties and Schools of Information Technology.
  • 02 Departments of Computer Science and Computing.
  • 03 Colleges of Engineering and Technology.
  • 04 Universities and higher education institutions.
  • 05 Research institutes and academic organizations in related disciplines.

Membership Categories

Founding Members

Institutions participating in the establishment and initial development of the consortium.

Institutional Members

Universities, faculties, schools, and departments officially joining the consortium.

Associate Members

Academic and research organizations supporting the consortium's objectives.

Honorary Members

Distinguished scholars and academic leaders recognized for their contributions to international academic cooperation.

Why Join TIIS?

Expand Your Academic Network

Connect with academic leaders, researchers, and institutions across different countries.

Develop Institutional Partnerships

Explore opportunities for academic cooperation, joint programs, and international initiatives.

Enhance Research Collaboration

Build connections for joint research projects, publications, and scientific activities.

Participate in International Events

Engage in conferences, workshops, institutional meetings, and academic exchange programs.

Share Institutional Experiences

Exchange best practices in curriculum development, teaching, research management, and quality assurance.

Increase International Visibility

Promote your institution and strengthen its presence within the global academic community.
