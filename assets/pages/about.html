About TIIS

A shared space for academic collaboration.

The Technology and Information Institutions Consortium is a proposed international network for faculties, schools, and departments working in computing and information technology.

Vision

A connected academic community that advances knowledge, education, and research across borders.

Mission

Enable institutional partnerships, research exchange, academic mobility, and shared learning.

TIIS is currently a founding initiative. Its formal structure and membership will follow approval of the founding charter.
