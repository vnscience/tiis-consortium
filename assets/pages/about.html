About Us

Building a Global Community of Computing and IT Institutions

Proposed brand identity · tiis-consortium.org · Founding Academic Initiative.

The Technology and Information Institutions Consortium (TIIS Consortium) is an international academic network connecting higher education institutions, particularly faculties, schools, and departments specializing in Computing, Information Technology, Computer Science, and related disciplines.

TIIS provides a structured collaborative platform for academic leaders, faculty members, and researchers to exchange institutional experiences, develop joint research initiatives, support academic mobility, and co-organize international scientific events.

Our Vision

Global Academic Excellence

To become a dynamic international network connecting computing and IT institutions worldwide, fostering academic excellence, meaningful partnerships, and innovation in higher education.

Our Mission

Six Strategic Objectives

  • • Connect universities and academic institutions across countries and regions.
  • • Promote international cooperation in computing and IT education.
  • • Facilitate joint research, publications, and academic initiatives.
  • • Encourage faculty mobility, student exchange, and knowledge sharing.
  • • Organize and support international academic conferences and events.
  • • Build long-term institutional partnerships and a sustainable academic community.
