About TIIS

A connected community for intelligent infrastructures and systems.

The Transparent Intelligent Infrastructures and Systems (TIIS) Consortium is an international academic initiative connecting universities, faculties, schools, and research groups working across intelligent infrastructures, information systems, computing, and related disciplines.

Vision

A globally connected academic community advancing transparent, trustworthy, and intelligent systems.

Mission

Enable research collaboration, academic exchange, shared learning, and meaningful institutional partnerships.

How we collaborate

Research partnerships
Joint projects, publications, and research groups.

Academic exchange
Faculty and student mobility, visiting scholars, and shared learning.

Institutional dialogue
Meetings and visits to identify common priorities.

Events and workshops
Conferences, seminars, and focused knowledge exchange.

TIIS is in its founding initiative stage. Its formal structure and membership will follow approval of the founding charter.
